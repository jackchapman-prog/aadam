Ears
Make 4 details: 2 with white yarn, 2 with pink yarn
Crochet with turning rows:
1. 8 ch, turn, 7 sc, turn
2. 6 sc, turn
3. 5 sc, turn
4. 4 sc, turn
5. 3 sc, turn
6. 2 sc, fo
Put two parts together: put the pink part on top.
Crochet with turning rows through both details.
1. 12 sc, turn
2. 12 sc
Leave lon tail for sewing

Head sculpting
Insert a long needle with a strong thread through point 1 and pull it
through point 2, through point 3 enter the needle back and pull it
again through point 1, leaving threads for tightening.
Repeat the same for the second eye.
Tighten the threads well.
Next, insert the needle into point 1 and pull it through point 4, insert
again into point 1.
Tie the pulled ends of the thread into several tight knots. Cut the
thread, hide the ends.

-- 8 of 9 --

Head decoration
With a black cotton yarn embroider
eyebrows andeyelashes.
With a pink cotton yarn embroider muzzle.
Sew the ears to the head.
Sew the head to the body.
Sew the tail to the body on the row 9-10.
Сut the monofilament into lengths of 13-15 cm,
fasten it on the muzzle with a knot.
