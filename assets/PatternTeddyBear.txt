Abbreviations:
1. MR – magic ring;
2. SC – single crochet;
3. SL ST – slip stitch;
4. INC – increase (2 sc in one stitch);
5. DEC – decrease (2 sc together);
6. CH – chain;
7. DC – double crochet;
8. HDC – half double crochet;
9. HDC2TOG – crochet 2 hdc together
10. * - number of repetitions in the round;
11. (N) – number of stitches in the round.
Note!
The toy is crocheted in a spiral; at the end of each round, place a
marker - a thread of a contrasting color.
What is a stitch marker?
https://vk.com/video/@zefirkavlg?z=video-178489914_4562

You do NOT need to make a turning chain at the end of the round,
otherwise you will end up with an unsightly seam! If you are just starting
to crochet, count how many stitches you get in each round. Be careful and
patient, and you will definitely succeed!
A lighter is needed to further strengthen the fittings. I'll tell you about

Design:
After we have sewn all pieces, make a needle sculpting. Insert the
needle into the opening on the head, at point “A”, then exit through point
“B”, then insert at point “C” and exit through point “D”, then into “D” and
again pull the needle out into the hole of the head - “A”. Gently tighten the
snout, tie the thread into a knot and secure it to the back loop. Repeat the
tightening 2 more times. Don't forget to watch the video!
https://youtu.be/LTva4bW1aog
Then you can shape the snout, use a brown thread to form the muzzle,
tighten it and tie the ends behind the back loop of the round, then use the
same thread to embroider eyebrows. Fix again and hide the ends inside
the head.
Eyes. Take the main yarn, embroider the upper eyelid, then embroider
the whites of the eyes with a white thread.

Cap:
Work with DC or HDC stitches. If you want it longer, then work with DC, if
you want it shorter, then work with HDC. If you want a very small cap, you
can also crochet it with SC.
1. ch48, join in a circle! Work in a spiral!
2. 48 SC, next work with hdc and dc.
3. 48 hdc
4. (6 hdc, hdc2tog)*6 (42)
5-6. 42 hdc
7. (5 hdc, hdc2tog)*6 (36)
8-9. 36 hdc
10. (4 hdc, hdc2tog)*6 (30)
11-14. 30 hdc
15.(3 hdc, hdc2tog)*6 (24)
