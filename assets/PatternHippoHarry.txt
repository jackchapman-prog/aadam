Skills required
Crocheting in spiral, magic ring, chain, slip stitch,
single crochet stitch, increasing and decreasing.
Notes
• I used the Yarn Under technique for this pattern
(see ‘’Tips’’ section).
• Work in a spiral, do not join rounds or turn your
work, unless instructed otherwise in the pattern.
• Use a stitch marker or a piece of yarn to mark the
beginning of a round. Move the marker up after
completing each round.
• The pictures in this pattern were taken using
worsted weight yarn.
Difficulty
Intermediate
Contact – Follow me!
Materials
• Yarn – see pictures below
• Safety eyes – see pictures below
• Crochet hook – see pictures below
• Some leftover yarn in white, pink
and black or dark grey (for the eyes,
teeth, cheeks and eyebrows)
• Polyester fiberfill for stuffing
• Yarn needle, scissors, stitch marker
Abbreviations
• ch = chain
• BLO = crochet in the back loops only
• dec = invisible decrease
(see ‘’Tips’’ section)
• inc = increase – crochet two single
crochet stitches in the same stitch
• mr of n sc = crochet n (number)
single crochet stitches into the
magic ring (see ‘’Tips’’ section)
• sc = single crochet stitch
• sc n = crochet n single crochet
stitches (or ch, hdc etc.), one in each
stitch
• (sc 4, inc) x n = repeat the pattern
between parentheses n times
• sl st = slip stitch
• st(s) = stitch(es)
• (36) = number of stitches in a round
after finishing the round

MAGIC RING
A magic ring is a way to begin crocheting in round by crocheting the first round into an adjustable loop
and then pulling the loop tight. See how I make my magic ring of 6 sc here: https://youtu.be/wdVIx0MpuVc
Alternatively, you can chain 2, crochet n single crochet stitches into the second chain from hook.
Let’s start!
Muzzle | Head
You will start by working around the foundation chain for the muzzle.
1. Ch 10, starting in the 2nd chain from the hook: inc, sc 7, make 4 sc in the last ch.
Now working on the other side of the ch: sc 7, inc in last. (22)
Do not join and keep working in spiral.
2. (inc, sc 4) x2, inc x2, (sc 4, inc) x2 (28)*
3. Image 1. (inc, sc 2, inc, sc 6, inc, sc 2, inc) x2 (36)
4-8. sc around (5 rounds) (36)
9. sc, dec, (sc 4, dec) x5, sc 3 (30)
10. (sc 3, dec) x6 (24)
11. Image 2. (dec, sc 2) x6 (18)
Stuff the muzzle and keep stuffing the head as you go.
12. (inc, sc) x9 (27)
13. (sc 2, inc) x9 (36)
14. (sc 5, inc) x6 (42)
15-21. sc around (7 rounds) (42)
Images 3-4. Secure the eyes between rounds 13-14, about 7 sts apart. Place a sewing pin centered with
the muzzle to identify the middle of the face. Then place the eyes on both sides.
Images 5-8. Embroider the cheeks with a strand of pink yarn. Tie a knot inside the head after each
embroidery.
Images 9-12. Embroider the eyebrows with a strand of dark grey (or black) yarn.
*Beginner Help! If you are new to
reading patterns, here’s what round
2 means.
Follow steps 1-3 to complete round
2 and keep working on the muzzle.
Step 1: Repeat twice (inc, sc 4). So:
inc, sc, sc, sc, sc, inc, sc, sc, sc, sc
Step 2: inc twice. So: inc, inc
Step 3: Repeat twice (sc 4, inc). So:
sc, sc, sc, sc, inc, sc, sc, sc, sc, inc

Nostrils | Teeth
Image 25. For the nostrils, place a total of 4 sewing pins on the top of the muzzle. They should be
between rounds 5-6, and count 6 stitch holes (red dots) apart. The white line is the center of the face.
Images 26-27. Flip the head around so the eyes are close to you. For the right nostril, insert the hook to
the right of the pin 1 and out through pin 1. Join a new strand of main color yarn and pull through.
Images 28-29. Ch 3 and sl st into the next st (in point 1 and out through point 2).
Image 30. Cut the yarn and pull it through to fasten off. Hide the yarn tails.
Image 31. Now, insert your hook into pin 3 and out through pin 4. Join a new strand of yarn and ch 3.
Image 32. Sl st by inserting the hook into pin 4 and out next to it. Cut the yarn, pull through and hide the
yarn tails.
Now, if you want to add teeth, you will follow the same steps as for the nostrils, but with white yarn.
Images 33-35. The teeth are under the muzzle, between rounds 3-4, and count 3 stitch holes apart this
time.

See ‘’Tips’’ section. Fold the arm in half and make 3 sc crocheting through
both sides. Cut the yarn and weave in the end (you will not need to sew
the arms).
Legs (make 2)
You will start by working around the foundation chain like you did for the muzzle.

inc in the last ch.
2. (inc, sc 4, inc) x2 (16)
3. BLO: sc around (16)
4. sc 14, dec (15)
5. sc 5, dec x3, sc 4 (12)
6. sc 4, dec x2, sc 4 (10)
7. (inc, sc) x5 (15)
Stuff the legs.
*For the 1st leg only: place a stitch marker in the 11th stitch of round 7. Leave a short yarn tail and
make an invisible fasten off (see ‘’Tips’’ section).
*For the 2nd leg only: do NOT cut the yarn and continue to the body section.
