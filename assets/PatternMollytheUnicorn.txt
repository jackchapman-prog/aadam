Materials
You can use any materials, which are convenient for you. The main
thing is to choose the hook correctly. I use plastic joints as a fixing arms
and legs. You can also use plastic joins or cotter pin fastening. In
addition, you can just sew the details or use the other kind of fastening
(thread fastening, button fastening).
✓ Plush yarn for unicorn (primary color). I used Himalaya Dolphin
Baby (100g/120m = 131yd/3.5oz)
✓ Plush yarn for hooves (secondary color)
✓ Plush yarn of different colors for mane and tail.
✓ Thinner yarn for horn. I used Kartopu Elegance (100g/180m =
131yd/6.3oz)
✓ Durable thread of similar color for sewing
✓ Hooks. I used 3 mm for
body, 4 mm for mane and
tail, 2 mm for horn.
✓ Plastic joints or fasteners
cotter pins and disks of
suitable size (4 sets) (I used
plastic joints 25 mm for
legs, 20 мм for arms)

Abbreviations
✓ MR – Magic Ring
✓ sc – single crochet
✓ inc – increase (crochet 2sc in one stitch)
✓ dec – decrease (crochet 2sc together)
✓ (…)* - what is in brackets, repeat the specified number of times
✓ ch - chain
All the details are crocheted in a spiral.
Legs (2 pcs)
Start with hoof-colored yarn.
1. 6 sc in MR
2. 6 inc (12)
3. (sc, inc)*6 (18)
4. (2 sc, inc)*6 (24)
5. (3 sc, inc)*6 (30)
Change on primary color.
6-8. 30 sc (3 rows)
9. 11 sc, 6 dec, 7 sc (24)
10. 7 sc, 6 dec, 5 sc (18)
11-13. 18 sc (3 rows)
14. 3 sc, dec, 9 sc, dec, 2 sc (16)
Start stuff the leg.
15. 3 sc, dec, 8 sc, dec, 1 sc (14)
16. 2 sc, dec, 7 sc, dec, 1 sc (12)
17-18. 12 sc (2 rows)

Points A, B, C, D are located between rounds 8 and 9. Point A – 1
sc after last inc, point B – 3 sc to the left. Point C – 1 sc before first inc,
point D – 3 sc to the right (PICTURE 1).
Cut a long piece of yarn in the same
color you used for crocheting the head,
thread the yarn needle and insert it from the
bottom of the head (where the head will sew
to the body) (PICTURE 2), push it at point A
(PICTURE 3) and enter back at point B. Push
the needle out from the bottom of our head,
near the initial point of entry (PICTURE 4).
Pull both tails out gently, tighten, tail with a
double knot and hide the loose ends inside
the head.
Repeat what you did in pictures 2-4 for right eye with points C
and D (PICTURE 5 and 6).

Assembly
1. Sew the ears to the head.
2. Stuff and sew the horn to the
head.
3. Embroider the brows. I used
the same thread as the horn.
4. Embroider upper eyelid with black
thin thread.
5. Sew the head to the body.
6. Sew the mane to the head.
7. Sew all the spirals for the tail
together.
