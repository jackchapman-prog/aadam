Amigurumi Pattern Maker

Enter gauge, yarn, and hook. Type an animal. The app sketches it as spheres, cylinders, and cones, then maps those solids to sc / inc / dec rounds — the same notebook math amigurumi designers use.

Your gauge

These drive the stitch math. Changing yarn weight below also updates these to a typical amigurumi gauge for that yarn (you can override). Pattern refreshes as you type.

Yarn & hook
Build style

These steer construction. “Auto” keeps the owned style for that animal (e.g. Cat → Kitten Kaya sitting + JAYG). Face construction chooses sphere+patch vs continuous snout→head when the animal supports both (e.g. otter).

Animal

Type any animal. The app builds pieces the way a crochet artist would for that animal, then sizes them to your height.

If the photo clearly shows an animal, the app detects it and adjusts proportions (legs/neck/body/head). Total height in inches still comes from you — photos have no ruler.
