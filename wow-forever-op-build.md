## Why Enhancement is the OP pick

1. **Rage of the Farseer** — personal Bloodlust: +30% melee and spell haste for 25 seconds  
2. **Maelstrom Weapon** — melee builds stacks that make **Lightning Bolt** cheap / fast / often instant  
3. **Stormstrike** — nature damage amp on the target + Improved Stormstrike cooldown resets  
4. **2H axes/maces** — baseline (no talent tax like Classic)

## Combat loop (simple)

1. Keep **weapon imbue** + **Lightning Shield** up (Windfury / Flametongue per gear guide later — not Rockbiter in raids).  
2. **Stormstrike** on cooldown.  
3. Melee → stack **Maelstrom Weapon** → dump **Lightning Bolt**.  
4. Weave **Earth Shock** / **Flame Shock** as mana allows.  
5. Pop **Rage of the Farseer** on burn phases, big pulls, or Lust windows.

```
Melee swings → Maelstrom stacks → Lightning Bolt
     ↑                ↑
Stormstrike (nature amp)    Rage of the Farseer (burst)
```

## Equipment (what to wear)

**Honest caveat:** Forever has **no reliable named BiS list yet** (new loot + itemization). Use rules below until Wowhead/Icy Veins publish Forever Enhance lists after beta.

### Armor & weapons

| Slot / type | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Armor | **Mail** at 40+ (Leather while leveling is normal) |
| Weapon | Highest **DPS 2H** you can use (axe if Orc for Axe Spec; mace if Dwarf for Mace Spec). Dual wield is **not confirmed** in Forever yet — plan on **one 2H**. |
| Shield | Optional for tough solo pulls; swap back to 2H for damage |

### Stat priority (Enhancement DPS)

Because Forever **unifies Hit and Crit** (melee + spell share the same stats), hybrid gear is much friendlier than Classic.

1. **Hit** — until you stop missing (raid soft cap still TBD; stack Hit early)  
2. **Strength / Attack Power** — melee damage  
3. **Intellect** — Mana + **Mental Dexterity** (→ AP) + **Mental Quickness** (→ spell power for bolts)  
4. **Crit** — Flurry procs + bigger bolts  
5. **Agility** — crit / dodge (nice, not first)  
6. **Stamina** — solo survivability (take it on quest greens freely)

**Avoid obsessing over pure caster cloth** or pure Strength plate with no Int — Enhance wants the mix.

### Weapon imbues

| Situation | Imbue |
| --- | --- |
| Solo questing | **Rockbiter** if you need threat/survivability; otherwise **Flametongue** or **Windfury** (test which kills faster for you) |
| Dungeons / raids (DPS) | **Windfury** or **Flametongue** — **not Rockbiter** (Spirit Weapons: no Rockbiter = −30% threat) |
| Always | Keep **Lightning Shield** up |

### Enchants & consumables (Classic-style targets)

Until Forever recipes finalize, aim for the usual melee DPS package:

- Weapon: crusader / agility / strength style enchants (whatever is BiS on live lists)  
- Consumables: **Mana potions**, strength/AP food, flasks/elixirs when raiding  
- Totems: Strength of Earth, Windfury Totem (if no WF imbue conflict rules), Grace of Air, Searing/Magma for trash — don’t drop every totem for every solo mob

### Gear while leveling (practical)

- Quest rewards with **Str + Int + Stam** or **AP + Int**  
- Dungeon blues with weapon DPS upgrades beat tiny stat upgrades  
- At 60: run dungeons → then raid tiers; follow the first Forever Enhance BiS guide that cites **actual Forever item IDs**

## Professions (recommended for your playstyle)

You solo a lot, then raid. Best default:

### Locked recommendation: **Herbalism + Alchemy**

| Why |
| --- |
| Free **Mana / healing potions** while questing = less downtime and fewer deaths |
| Flasks/elixirs for raids later without buying everything |
| Herbs sell well at launch (gold for mounts, repairs, respecs) |
| Fits a first character who is often alone |

**Secondary skills (always take these):**

- **Cooking** — food buffs + Forever food can give a small XP bonus while leveling  
- **First Aid** — bandages between fights  
- **Fishing** — optional; feeds Cooking

### Strong alternatives

| Pair | Best if… |
| --- | --- |
| **Mining + Engineering** | You want **max raid/PvP utility** (bombs, sappers, gadgets). Classic Enhance “try-hard” pair. Mining also gives **+5% health** in Forever. |
| **Skinning + Leatherworking** | You want to **craft your own** leather/mail upgrades while leveling and save gold |

**Beginner path:** start **Herbalism + Alchemy**. At 60, if you join a serious raid team and Engineering is still strong, **drop Herbalism → Engineering** (keep Alchemy) or go full **Mining + Engineering**.
